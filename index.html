Our story

A family kitchen that's been feeding Dearborn since around 1957.

Lim's Garden has been a quiet fixture on Carlysle Street for nearly seventy years. Multiple generations have worked the kitchen, and many of our regulars have been eating here longer than some of us have been alive. The menu hasn't changed much — and that's the point.

We cook family-style Cantonese and Chinese-American comfort food. Generous portions, fresh ingredients, no shortcuts. Most combination dinners feed two people. Our fried rice is the kind people drive across town for.

We're takeout only — call ahead, swing by, and your order will be hot and waiting. Cash and card both fine.
